# Attribution for the photographs in this folder

These ten photographs are the ones Figures 1 and 2 of the notebook read, so both figures redraw
without the full download. Each is a specimen photograph from the Natural History Museum Data
Portal, dataset Collection specimens (https://data.nhm.ac.uk/dataset/collection-specimens),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0).

The photographs are modified: labels masked. The labels carry collectors' handwriting and names. In
each photograph, downloaded on 13 October 2026, everything beyond the crop rule's box is painted
grey, on the side where the rule found the labels and on the side where a tray of that shape keeps
them. Nothing inside the box is painted, so the specimen is not, and the ruler is painted only
where it runs on past the box on a label side. `python scripts/build_working_set.py mask` made each
copy from the original and saved it as a JPEG again, with the original's own quantisation tables
and chroma subsampling. `masks.csv` here gives the box and the label side each photograph was
masked by. The SHA-256 in each photograph's row of `data/manifest_fixed_20261013.csv` is the
original's, so no copy here matches it.

The Museum cannot warrant the quality or accuracy of the data, and this work has no endorsement,
affiliation, support or approval from the Museum.
